This well-appointed, one-owner 2017 Lexus RX 350 F SPORT Series 2 has been thoroughly inspected and is in excellent condition, ready for immediate delivery.

A 3.5L V6 engine producing 295 horsepower and 267 lb-ft of torque pairs with an 8-speed automatic transmission and all-wheel drive . Fuel consumption is rated at 12.2 L/100 km city and 9.0 L/100 km highway . Properly equipped, towing capacity is rated at 3,500 lbs .

The F SPORT Series 2 builds upon the F SPORT Series 1 with performance enhancements including Adaptive Variable Suspension (AVS), Vehicle Dynamics Integrated Management (VDIM), and an F SPORT-tuned steering system . The cabin features heated and ventilated F SPORT leather-trimmed front sport seats, a heated leather-wrapped F SPORT steering wheel with paddle shifters, aluminum sport pedals, and an LFA-inspired digital instrument cluster .

A 12.3-inch navigation display includes Lexus Remote Touch interface, Bluetooth connectivity, and a 12-speaker premium audio system . Additional amenities include a power moonroof, dual-zone automatic climate control, power rear door, and heated power-folding side mirrors with memory and reverse tilt-down .

Lexus Safety System+ provides pre-collision with pedestrian detection, all-speed dynamic radar cruise control, lane departure alert with steering assist, and automatic high beams . Blind spot monitoring with rear cross-traffic alert, intuitive parking assist, and 10 airbags enhance peace of mind .

Additional features include 20-inch F SPORT alloy wheels, triple-beam LED headlamps, LED fog lamps, roof rails, and a block heater.

This RX 350 F SPORT Series 2 delivers an exceptional combination of V6 performance, sport-tuned handling, and premium appointments.
